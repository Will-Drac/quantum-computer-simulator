gates on the bottom left
    when you hover them, it shows a preview of what the gate will do on the bloch sphere:
        for single qbit gates:
            shows a bunch of vectors on the bloch sphere and animates their rotation to where they will be after the gate
            if the gate has inputs, first it animates to the position with the inputs at 0, then it animates the actual inputs
            all along, it shows the input values and the power of the matrix being displayed at this time
            the animation will be showing the bloch sphere with gate X^t t from [0, 1]
                we can store a lot of what's needed to calculate the power for performance

        for multiple qbit gates:
            for each pure state which might be the input, it shows the output
            no animation, too hard
            essentially just shows what can be seen by looking at the matrix

circuit builder at the top left
    you can give it as many qbits as you want
        qbits can be grouped together and collapsed?

    same hover gate thing as above

    updates on every edit

    editable seed
    OR, you can manually choose the outcome of the measurement/reset for debugging purposes

    inspect mode
        runs through the layers of gates step by step
        animates the bloch spheres on each line of the circuit for each layer so you can see how the qbits move throughout
            maybe just lazy animation where the vector moves linearly from initial to final

state vector on the top right
    for each basis state:
        probability as a bar graph
        phase and amplitude as a phasor
